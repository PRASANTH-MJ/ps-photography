How booking works

  1. Message us your date

    Tell us the shoot type, date and city. We reply within a few hours with availability.

  2. Plan on a call

    We go through rituals, locations, outfits and must-have shots, then send a fixed quote.

  3. Pay 30% to confirm

    Your date is blocked only for you. The balance is due on the shoot day.

  4. Get your photos

    Preview in 72 hours, full gallery in 3–4 weeks, album after you choose the photos.

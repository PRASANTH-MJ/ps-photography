Questions couples and parents ask

Do you travel outside Chennai?

Yes. We cover all of Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and destination weddings. Travel and stay are added at cost.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings in peak muhurtham months, 3–6 months ahead. Baby and model shoots usually need 1 week.

Will we get all the photos, or only edited ones?

You get every usable photo, colour-corrected, plus a set of fully retouched favourites.

Is the newborn studio safe for babies?

The studio is heated, sanitised before each session, and props are washed. We never force poses, and a parent stays within arm's reach.

Can we pay in instalments?

Yes: 30% to confirm the date, the rest on the shoot day. UPI, card and bank transfer accepted.
